# V3 prospective exploratory protocol

V3 starts from V2 commit `2a090b4f50a02c2eda89b8ee303102d608f7a390`. V1/V2 files and split membership remain byte-identical. V2 PR #1 remains unmerged; V3 targets its branch. These are cross-sectional cardiovascular labels; neither future events nor external clinical validation. Person linkage and dataset reuse rights remain uncertain.

## Evaluation and finite budget

Only original V1 training records are eligible. Three new stratified group-disjoint outer folds are fixed before fitting; groups hash the minimally cleaned predictors, excluding label and ID. This population and age grouping have already informed earlier research: all V3 findings are exploratory internal evidence. A new split does not erase that dependence.

Classification uses an inner grouped 60/20/20 fit/checkpoint-stop/candidate-selection split within each outer-training fold. This is nested holdout selection, not three-fold inner CV. Each of 28 registered candidates is fitted once per outer fold. The best new neural candidate and best boosted-tree candidate receive one additional paired seed per fold: 90 fits. All candidates use the same roles. No refit on stopping or selection records. Equal tree budgets: two configurations per library; neural mechanism controls have explicitly different family budgets. Each candidate has a fixed maximum epoch/round budget; AP checkpointing uses exact non-interpolated sklearn AP. Scratch epoch zero is diagnostic and ineligible. A strict-cleaning sensitivity uses common strict evaluation records and is excluded from primary selection.

Age splines are fitted only on training data; nonlinear products add no independent measurement. Quantile normalization is train-fitted. Shuffled missingness flags and common strict-clean sensitivity test whether data-quality signals drive gains. Gate, residual-depth, identity and parameter-matched flat controls identify particular mechanisms without assuming clinical interpretation. BCE is primary, focal gamma 2 and BCE plus pairwise softplus weights .01/.1 are fixed alternatives. FT is a fixed official-implementation reference. Ensembles use inner selection predictions (single held-out inner OOF block) for convex weights, never outer labels.

Transfer is INTERNAL AGE-SUBGROUP ADAPTATION: source age <55, target >=55. Three target outer folds, with disjoint source fit/stop/reference roles. Within each target outer-training pool, 40% supplies fitting examples, 30% selector calibration, 10% checkpoint pool, 20% model-selection pool, group-disjoint. Fitting and selector each use 25, 250 or 1,000 positives and the same negatives. Checkpoint and model-selection sets each contain 50 positives and 50 negatives. These deliberately balanced development roles differ from the natural target prevalence. New source initializations use seeds 101/202/303, one per fold. Source preprocessing is fitted only on source fit and held fixed through adaptation.

Thirty fitting methods per budget/fold, three sources and nine small-stopping-set sensitivity fits: 282 transfer fits. Source-only is a checkpoint reuse, not a new fit. Three smoke fits are allowed: total initial budget 375, hard ceiling 400 including successful diagnostics. Failures are separately recorded and resumable. No budget expansion after evaluation. P100/P500 are omitted to prioritize mandatory controls.

## Prespecified transfer mechanisms

V2's exact seven-module evidence and logical rule are imported unchanged: valid geometry and `U < .75 OR (p_MMD <= .05 AND R < .8 AND U < 1)`. Invalid geometry abstains. Head is always trainable. MMD uses four Gaussian bandwidths, 199 permutations and 128-per-domain sample cap; class geometry uses all available selector labels and 199 bootstrap replicates. Source-reference fitting uses its separate labels. No V2 target-evaluation predictions train or select any policy.

L2-SP penalizes the sum of squared distances to a detached, immutable source backbone (head excluded), strengths .01 and 1. Module-specific penalties use .01 + .99 times invariance: geometry `1-clip(1-U,0,1)`; MMD `p_MMD`; combined average. Invalid geometry receives preservation penalty 1. Shuffling the same seven penalties is a control. AdamW backbone decay is zero during adaptation; source preservation is explicit.

Soft adaptation uses `theta=theta_source + cap*delta`, with per-module delta projected onto an L2 ball of radius `.1*max(||theta_source||,1)`. Fixed caps: uniform .5, evidence `.1+.8*(.7*clip(1-U)+.3*(1-p)*clip(1-R))`, invalid geometry zero, random U(.1,.9), and shuffled evidence. The learned radial variant has cap 1 and learns its effective radial gate `||delta||/radius`. This constrained radial decomposition has no independently rescalable learned gate/delta pair. It is distinct from a free sigmoid-gate model; cap differences are part of the intervention. Both nominal caps and effective radial gates/drift are recorded. L2-SP .01 applies to effective drift for soft methods.

Adaptation uses AP stopping, epoch zero eligible, maximum 80 epochs, patience 12, AdamW lr .0003, batch128. Source uses AP stopping max120/patience16. Every retained model is compared to its exact source initialization; frozen tensors and source digests are verified.

## Primary hypothesis and supervision accounting

For each fold/budget, choose the best of ten registered new policies using ONLY the 100 model-selection labels, maximizing AP (ties Brier then method ID). Primary outcome at P1000: AP difference of this selection procedure versus full fine-tuning with matched total target-label access. Secondary AP/AUROC differences versus head, logical, random and source; P25/P250 are secondary. The candidate-selection procedure consumes fitting + selector + stopping + model-selection roles: `4P+200` unique labels (300/1200/4200). Full-cost fits the fitting, selector and model-selection union, with the same 100 stopping labels. Target-only cost controls receive the same accessible labels. Fixed-method actual costs are also reported. Reusing a label in multiple roles is counted once; candidate-search supervision is not hidden. Compute budgets differ and are recorded.

Seven single-module counterfactuals assess selector evidence versus actual **development-selection** AP benefit, without using outer outcomes to redesign the policy. Learned meta-selection is NOT EXECUTED: three overlapping age tasks cannot provide independent meta-training and meta-evaluation populations. Additional view and random controls are descriptive.

A secondary P25 sensitivity uses 20 stopping labels (10/class), rerunning head, full-cost and the already selected new policy; the other 80 stopping labels are withheld from all three and removed from their label accounting. Selection still consumes its registered 100 labels. No claim of '25-label transfer'.

## Locked analysis

All checkpoint hashes, model selections, ensemble weights, code hashes and protocol hashes are locked before outer prediction. Evaluation happens once per track; reruns only reproduce identical locked artifacts. Paired group bootstrap (1,000 draws stratified by outer fold, shared predictions) reports conditional 95% intervals for delta AP/AUROC. These omit algorithm-selection, source-training and cohort-sampling uncertainty; shared source participants, seeds/folds/draws and uncertain person linkage prevent treating fits as independent populations. Multiple secondary comparisons are exploratory. A .005 AP difference is the prespecified practical reference; small favorable estimates alone do not establish superiority. Ranking, calibration, fixed-threshold classification, parameter drift and negative transfer are reported separately. Threshold .5 is descriptive, not clinical advice.

No external transfer is executed without verified endpoint harmonization, permissions and independent cohorts. No raw data, predictions, checkpoints or preprocessing objects are published. Public outputs are original code, synthetic tests and nonreconstructive aggregate metrics/figures; unknown Kaggle licence is not an unrestricted redistribution licence.
